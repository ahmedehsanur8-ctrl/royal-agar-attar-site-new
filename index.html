From tree to oil

Made in ourown factory

Oud oil comes from the dark resin an agar tree forms to heal itself. The wood is sorted by hand, soaked for 2–2.5 months, then distilled slowly in our degs in runs of about 10 days. Nothing is added after distillation.

    Questions buyers ask

    Straightanswers

    Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. We usually reply the same day.

    01

    How can I check the quality?

    Quality
    Order a 6 ml or 12 ml sample and judge it yourself. You are welcome to visit the factory in Sujanagar and watch the distillation. On orders of half a litre to one litre, we can arrange a lab test. Ask us when you order.
    02

    Is anything added to the oil?

    Process
    No. Nothing is added after distillation. Each grade is bottled from our own degs, and grades are not mixed with each other.
    03

    What is the minimum order?

    Orders
    Samples come in 6 ml and 12 ml. Wholesale starts at 120 g, which is about 10 tola. Larger and repeat orders are welcome. Churon grades can be reserved from the next batch.
    04

    Can you ship to my country?

    Export
    Not yet. Agarwood oil and chips need CITES export permits, and ours are not in place yet, so today we sell within Bangladesh. International buyers are still welcome to message us, ask questions, or visit the factory, and we will tell you as soon as we can export.
    05

    Where does your wood come from?

    Origin
    We buy agarwood from growers and suppliers across Sylhet. Most of it comes from nailed agar trees, where iron nails help the tree form resin. We sort it by hand and distill it ourselves in Sujanagar.
